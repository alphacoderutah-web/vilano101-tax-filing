Last reviewed while building skill: 2026-08-12.

These are verification targets, not permanent substitutes for current official rules.

## Florida DOR

Official pages:

- `https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/taxesfees/Pages/sales_tax.aspx`
- `https://floridarevenue.com/Forms_library/current/dr15n.pdf`
- `https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/taxesfees/Pages/discretionary.aspx`
- `https://pointmatch.floridarevenue.com/General/DiscretionarySalesSurtaxRates.aspx`

Current DR-15 instruction controls verified 2026-08-12:

- Returns/payments due on the 1st, late after the 20th following the reporting period.
- Electronic payment cutoff is earlier than the delinquency date; current instructions specify confirmation by 5 p.m. ET on the business day before the 20th.
- File a return for each reporting period even when no tax is due.

Before each return verify:

1. state sales-tax treatment of transient rentals;
2. county discretionary surtax rate/effective date;
3. marketplace-provider treatment;
4. current DR-15 line/form instructions;
5. current electronic-payment calendar deadline.

## Flagler County TDT

Official page:

- `https://www.flaglertax.gov/tourist-development-tax/`

Current controls verified 2026-08-12:

- 5% TDT on taxable rental receipts/gross monthly revenue.
- Taxable examples include room rate, cleaning fees, pet fees, traveler service fees.
- Non-taxable examples include damage deposits and optional travel insurance.
- Due on the 1st following the reporting period and delinquent after the 20th.
- Return required even with zero tax collected.
- Timely Tourist Express filing/payment may allow 2.5% of first $1,200 tax due, max $30.

Re-verify every period.

## St. Johns County TDT

Official county/tax collector sources:

- `https://www.stjohnstax.us/`
- `https://www.sjcfl.us/`

Current county materials confirm a 5-cent/5% bed/tourist tax on overnight/qualifying transient accommodations. Use the live tax collector portal for filing procedure, taxable-base instructions, allowances/fees, and due-date behavior.

## Utah lodging / transient-room tax

Official sources:

- `https://tap.utah.gov/`
- `https://tax.utah.gov/forms-pubs/pub-56/`
- `https://tax.utah.gov/business/sales-tax/sales-related-taxes/transient-room/`
- `https://tax.utah.gov/business/sales-tax/sales/rates/`
- `https://tax.utah.gov/online-services/tap-salestax/`
- `https://tax.utah.gov/tax-bulletins/`

Current controls verified 2026-08-12:

- Utah lodging amounts are generally subject to sales tax and transient room tax under Pub 56.
- Report sales tax on TC-62M or TC-62S and transient room tax on TC-62T.
- Current rate must be determined by location/outlet and effective period.
- Utah Bulletin 8-26: Washington County county transient-room tax increased from 4.25% to 4.5% effective July 1, 2026.
- Bulletin 8-26 specifies TC-62T filing beginning July 2026 for monthly filers, July-Sep 2026 for quarterly filers, and Jan-Dec 2026 for annual filers.

Do not hard-code a single combined St. George lodging rate. Verify city/outlet rates and any sales-tax changes each quarter.
